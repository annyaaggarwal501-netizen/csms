# Bulk photo prompts – for the Gemini / ChatGPT app (no API needed)

Work in **one chat**: send the **style message** first, then the product lines. The AI remembers the style, so every photo looks like the same photoshoot.

## Step 1 – send this style message once

```
I am making catalogue photos for my clothing shop. For every product I send you, create ONE separate image (never a collage) following these rules exactly:

STYLE
- A real photograph from a professional fashion e-commerce photoshoot. It must look like it was shot on a full-frame DSLR camera (85mm lens, f/2.8), not like AI art.
- NOT an illustration, NOT a cartoon, NOT a 3D render, NOT a painting, NOT a drawing.

MODEL (when the product is worn)
- A real Indian model with natural skin texture, natural face and hands (five fingers), a relaxed natural pose, light natural make-up.
- Full-length shot, the whole outfit visible from head to feet, model facing the camera or at a slight angle.
- Kids' products: an Indian child of about 6-8 years, happy natural expression, modest clothing.

PRODUCT
- The garment is the hero: real fabric texture, weave, folds, stitching, embroidery and print clearly visible and sharp.
- Colours exactly as I describe, true to life, no colour cast.

SETTING AND LIGHT
- A bright, softly lit pastel café with pink and lavender flowers, white marble tables and pastel striped walls; background softly blurred.
- Soft natural daylight from a window, gentle shadows, clean and premium look.

PRODUCT-ONLY ITEMS (marked [PRODUCT ONLY])
- No person. The item neatly arranged on a clean light pastel surface with a few soft flowers at the edge, top-down or straight-on, soft natural shadow, square 1:1 format, like a 100mm macro product photo.

FORMAT
- Worn products: vertical 4:5 portrait. Product-only items: square 1:1.
- No text, no letters, no price tags, no logo, no brand name, no watermark, no border, no frame.

Reply "Ready" and wait. I will then send the products one by one (or a few at a time). Make one image per product.
```

## Step 2 – send the products

Copy one batch (5 products) and send it. If the app joins them into one picture, send the lines **one at a time** instead.
Download each picture and name it with the product number (`1.jpg`, `2.jpg` …). If the chat gets long and photos start to look different, open a new chat and send the style message again.

If a photo looks fake, reply: *"Make photo N more realistic – like a real camera photo, natural skin and real fabric texture."*

## Step 3 – put them in the app
Products → 📷 **Bulk photos** → select all the files → Upload. `9.jpg` goes to product 9 automatically.

**Watermark:** the style message forbids text/logos/watermarks inside the picture. The Gemini app may still put its own small sparkle mark in a corner; ChatGPT does not add a visible mark. Note that these are sample pictures — photos of the real stock are best for customers.
