# Shop manager – quick guide for the Owner

Open the shop link on the phone or computer and log in. Everything is in the menu on the left (on a phone: ☰).

## Every day
| I want to… | Go to |
|---|---|
| Make a bill | **New bill** → scan the tag, or tap a product photo and choose colour + size → choose how the customer pays → **Complete Sale (F9)** → Print or WhatsApp |
| Keep a bill aside while serving someone else | **New bill** → **Hold bill (F8)**; later **Held bills** → Resume |
| Take back or exchange an item | **Returns & exchange** → type the bill number |
| Give an item on credit (udhaar) | At payment choose **Credit (udhaar)** (the customer must be chosen first) |
| Receive money for credit | **Credit (udhaar)** → click the customer → **Receive payment** |
| See today's sales | **Dashboard** |

## When new stock arrives
1. **Stock-in (purchase)** → choose the supplier, type the supplier's bill number → scan or search each item → enter the quantity → **Save stock-in**.
2. **Barcode labels** → *From a stock-in bill…* → choose that bill → **Print labels** (one label per piece). Stick them on the tags.
3. A completely new design? **Products → + Add product** first (name, category, sizes, colours, prices, photo), then do the stock-in.

## Each week / month
| I want to… | Go to |
|---|---|
| Sales, profit, GST, slow-moving stock, staff sales | **Reports** (choose the period; Excel or Print/PDF buttons at the top) |
| Correct stock (damaged, lost, counted wrong) | **Stock** → **Adjust** on that row |
| Start a sale / discount | **Offers → + New offer** (whole shop, a category or one product; start and end time) |
| See who did what | **Activity log** |

## People and settings
- **Staff & logins:** add a login for each staff member. Give them the temporary password; they choose their own at first login.
  If someone types the wrong password 5 times the login locks: click **Unlock**.
- **My account:** change your password and the **Owner PIN**. Staff need the PIN for discounts above the limit, late returns
  and voiding a bill – never tell it to staff.
- **Settings:** shop name, address, phone, bill footer, GST (on/off + GSTIN), staff discount limit, return days, loyalty points.

## Rules the app follows by itself
- Stock can never go below 0, and every piece in or out is recorded (Stock → click a row to see its history).
- Staff never see cost prices or profit.
- A bill can be voided only on the same day (Owner, or staff with the Owner PIN). Older bills: use Returns.
- WhatsApp messages go only to customers who agreed (consent is recorded on the customer page).
